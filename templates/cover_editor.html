Emblems · logo / badge slots

Up to two positioned images (publisher logo, series badge, author mark). Transparent PNGs work best. Fixed slots, not freeform placement.

This mini-cover is drawn to the trim size. Each box is an emblem's footprint at its set width (solid = has an image; dashed = an empty slot, add one to fill it). Faint dots are the other available slots. Emblem height follows the image's own proportions.
