Illustration library

Figures

Images here can be placed in any manuscript with a figure block. Use the filename as the src: ~~~ figure src="map.png" — the block's text becomes the caption.

Add an image

JPEG, PNG or GIF. Use the largest version you have — the engine scales it down to fit the page, and print wants roughly 300 pixels per inch of printed width.

Per-figure placement lives on the block: width="0.5" (fraction of the text width), align="left", full="yes" for its own page. Everything else — caption size, style, spacing — belongs to the style, under Figures in the style editor.
